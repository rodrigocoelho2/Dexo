WHAT CHANGED

Game bar: the game you're on, big, with its progress, plus the last games you opened, one click away. ALL GAMES opens the full list by generation, in smaller cards; it closes again after you pick one, and remembers if you leave it open.

Toolbar: one line instead of two. The Pokédex becomes a menu (HOME has ten of them), variants stay as buttons because you switch them often, and the checkboxes turn into icons.

NEW OPTIONS

VIEW → Overview shows every Pokémon in colour so you can browse the boxes, and your marks stay as a small ball on each slot. Switch back to Track and the silhouettes return; nothing is lost.

VIEW → Shiny sprites only changes the pictures. ORDER decides where forms go and can hide cosmetic ones. The yellow dot on VIEW tells you something isn't on its default.

Try it: pick another game in ALL GAMES, open VIEW and switch to Overview or Shiny, set the variant to Forms and play with ORDER. The box on the left follows every change (Kanto 001–030 with Alolan forms and Pikachu's caps as the example).
